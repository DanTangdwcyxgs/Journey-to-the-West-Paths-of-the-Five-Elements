# Chapter Plan & Narrative Timeline

> This document is the working chapter bible. It defines the order in which the five personal stories intersect with the shared Journey to the West timeline.

## 1. High-Level Structure

The campaign is divided into six narrative phases:

1. **Five Origins** — choose any protagonist and experience their early life.
2. **Canonical Convergence** — each origin reaches the character's recruitment-era event.
3. **Growing Party** — the traveling group expands according to the canonical pilgrimage order.
4. **Personal Memories** — after recruitment, individual stories can be revisited as flashbacks/perspective chapters, catching up with unfinished character material without breaking chronology.
5. **Shared Pilgrimage** — all five characters travel together through the major trials.
6. **Final Journey** — the pilgrimage becomes one continuous party campaign and all personal themes converge.

The player chooses the **first lens**, not the historical order of the world.

# 2. The Five Origins

## 2.1 Sun Wukong Route — "The One Who Refused Heaven"

**Theme:** freedom vs restraint

**Gameplay identity:** high power, transformation, aggressive exploration and breaking physical obstacles.

### WUK-01 — Stone Birth

- Flower Fruit Mountain.
- Birth from the stone and first exploration of the mountain.
- Tutorial combat against animals and minor spirits.
- Establish Wukong's curiosity, competitiveness and instinct to protect his troop.
- First hint that immortality is a question, not merely a power fantasy.

### WUK-02 — King Without Tomorrow

- Wukong becomes Monkey King.
- Aging monkeys create the first personal motivation: he refuses to accept that even kings die.
- Journey begins toward sages and immortals.
- Introduce early traversal and staff combat.

### WUK-03 — The Search for the Immortal Way

- Long travel to seek a master.
- Puti Zushi appears as the first truly mysterious teacher.
- Training is partially gameplay-driven: transformations, staff techniques, movement.
- Wukong learns that knowledge requires discipline.

### WUK-04 — Seventy-Two Forms

- Training arc.
- Small trials testing disguise, transformation and misdirection.
- The player learns that Wukong can solve situations without direct combat.
- Master warns against showing off his abilities.

### WUK-05 — Return to Flower Fruit Mountain

- Wukong returns stronger.
- Local threat gives him a chance to test what he has learned.
- He discovers that strength creates new responsibilities.
- Heaven notices him.

### WUK-06 — Dragon Palace

- Journey to the East Sea.
- Underwater dungeon.
- Ruyi Jingu Bang acquisition.
- First major equipment transformation.
- Dragon King conflict establishes the relationship between Wukong and celestial authority.

### WUK-07 — The Book of Death

- Wukong confronts the Underworld.
- Fights underworld guardians.
- Deletes the names of the monkey clan from the register.
- He believes he has defeated mortality itself.
- This is the beginning of his dangerous confidence.

### WUK-08 — Bimawen

- Heaven recruits Wukong to contain him.
- The player experiences Heaven as a bureaucratic world rather than a simple villain fortress.
- Wukong realizes he is being treated as an animal rather than an equal.
- Resigns and returns to Flower Fruit Mountain.

### WUK-09 — Qitian Dasheng

- Heaven attempts to appease Wukong with a title.
- He establishes himself as Great Sage Equal to Heaven.
- The player fights increasingly powerful celestial forces.
- The combat system should make Wukong feel extraordinarily strong here.

### WUK-10 — Peach Garden

- Peach Garden intrusion.
- Heavenly banquet conflict.
- Wukong's arrogance reaches its peak.
- Optional scenes reveal that some celestial beings genuinely fear him while others pity him.

### WUK-11 — Heaven's War

- Full celestial campaign.
- Erlang Shen becomes the major rival.
- Multi-stage battle and transformation showcase.
- Wukong is finally defeated through strategy, not raw damage.

### WUK-12 — Furnace of the Heavens

- Wukong is placed in Taishang Laojun's furnace.
- Fire Eyes are gained.
- The player discovers the furnace was not enough to destroy him.
- He escapes with greater perception but no greater freedom.

### WUK-13 — The Hand of Buddha

- Final rebellion against Heaven.
- The player is deliberately given fewer opportunities to win through brute force.
- Buddha's intervention ends the rebellion.
- Wukong is sealed beneath Five Elements Mountain.

### WUK-14 — Five Elements Mountain

- Time slows dramatically.
- Short playable interludes depict centuries of imprisonment.
- Wukong meets travelers, hears rumors of the world and realizes the world has moved on without him.
- End of origin route.

**Origin completion milestone:** `WUKONG_ORIGIN_COMPLETE`

**Narrative handoff:** Tang Sanzang's pilgrimage eventually reaches the mountain.

**Recruitment:** Wukong becomes a party member when Tang removes the seal.

## 2.2 Tang Sanzang Route — "The One Who Chose the Road"

**Theme:** faith vs reality

**Gameplay identity:** preparation, support, negotiation, purification, protection and risk management.

### TANG-01 — The Child Named Xuanzang

- Establish Tang's human identity before his religious identity.
- Family tragedy and questions of fate.
- The player learns that Tang's greatest power is his ability to continue despite vulnerability.

### TANG-02 — Entering the Monastery

- Buddhist training.
- Dialogue, ritual and simple support mechanics.
- Introduce the concept that knowledge can be stronger than force.

### TANG-03 — The Monk Who Would Go West

- Tang learns about the scriptures.
- He becomes convinced that someone must make the journey.
- Imperial court and pilgrimage mandate.

### TANG-04 — Guanyin's Test

- Tang encounters supernatural signs.
- He is offered several safer alternatives but chooses the pilgrimage.
- Establish relationship with Guanyin.

### TANG-05 — Leaving Chang'an

- The first road section.
- Tang is weak in direct battle and must survive through preparation and allies.
- He learns to read hostile environments.

### TANG-06 — A World Full of Demons

- Several short encounters establish how helpless a normal traveler is.
- Rumors of a powerful prisoner beneath Five Elements Mountain begin appearing.

### TANG-07 — Five Elements Mountain

- Main convergence chapter.
- Tang reaches the mountain.
- Meets Wukong.
- Wukong offers strength; Tang insists on discipline.
- Tang removes the seal.

**Milestone:** `TANG_MEETS_WUKONG`

**Result:** Wukong joins Tang's traveling party.

### TANG-08 — The Golden Headband

- Establish master-disciple conflict.
- Tang receives/activates the constraint mechanism.
- Wukong realizes strength alone cannot decide every conflict.

### TANG-09 — The First Road Together

- First true duo adventure.
- Party combat tutorial expands from solo to two characters.
- Tang starts becoming the moral center of the journey.

---

## 2.3 Bai Longma Route — "The One Who Became the Road"

**Theme:** identity vs duty

**Gameplay identity:** mobility, stance switching, elemental adaptation and delayed reveal.

### LONG-01 — Prince of the Dragon Palace

- Establish White Dragon as a prince rather than a mount.
- Dragon Court politics, family pressure and ancient traditions.
- Water traversal tutorial.

### LONG-02 — The Dragon Prince's Defiance

- Conflict with the dragon court.
- White Dragon rejects a role chosen for him.
- Foreshadow the later theme of becoming what the world needs.

### LONG-03 — The Crime

- The incident that leads to his punishment.
- Keep the exact moral circumstances nuanced rather than treating him as simply guilty.

### LONG-04 — Condemned to the Mortal World

- The dragon is sent to Eagle Sorrow Stream.
- His identity is hidden from ordinary travelers.
- Gameplay shifts toward ambushes and environmental survival.

### LONG-05 — The Pilgrim Approaches

- Tang's group arrives.
- White Dragon initially sees Tang as another passing human.
- Guanyin's intervention changes his fate.

### LONG-06 — White Horse

- Transformation into Bai Longma.
- The player temporarily loses the full combat kit.
- Longma functions as a mount and exploration ability.

**Milestone:** `BAI_LONGMA_RECRUITED`

### LONG-07 — The Rider and the Road

- Perspective chapter after recruitment.
- Bai Longma begins understanding Tang's purpose.
- Combat traces of the future dragon forms appear.

### LONG-08 — Awakening of the Dragon

- Later milestone unlocks the full transformation system.
- Horse → Dragon → other tactical forms become playable states.
- This is a mechanical reveal, not a simple equipment unlock.

---

## 2.4 Zhu Bajie Route — "The One Who Fell"

**Theme:** desire vs responsibility

**Gameplay identity:** heavy defense, aggression, food/resource systems and disruption.

### BAJ-01 — Marshal Tianpeng

- Bajie before his fall.
- Celestial military setting.
- Establish charm, appetite, pride and emotional impulsiveness.

### BAJ-02 — A Marshal's Vanity

- Bajie's relationship with power and recognition.
- Optional scenes show that his selfishness is mixed with genuine loyalty.

### BAJ-03 — The Fall

- Punishment by Heaven.
- Rebirth as a monstrous pig-like being.
- Combat identity changes dramatically.

### BAJ-04 — A Life That Does Not Fit

- Bajie struggles to live among mortals.
- He wants comfort, companionship and respect but cannot obtain them normally.

### BAJ-05 — Gaojiazhuang

- Bajie becomes entangled with the Gao family.
- This section should be character-heavy and not reduce the situation to a joke.

### BAJ-06 — The Monkey Arrives

- Wukong and Tang arrive.
- Bajie recognizes that Wukong is operating on a completely different level.
- Rivalry begins.

### BAJ-07 — The Fight at Gaojiazhuang

- Major recruitment battle.
- Bajie's defensive mechanics are fully introduced.
- He has a genuine chance to reject the pilgrimage.

### BAJ-08 — A New Name

- Bajie accepts the journey reluctantly.
- His recruitment is an emotional compromise, not instant friendship.

**Milestone:** `ZHU_BAJIE_RECRUITED`

### BAJ-09 — The Third Seat

- First party camp scene with Tang, Wukong and Bajie.
- Establish comic conflict without making Bajie incompetent.

---

## 2.5 Sha Wujing Route — "The One Who Waited"

**Theme:** guilt vs redemption

**Gameplay identity:** control, water, endurance, defensive counterplay.

### SHA-01 — General of Heaven

- Establish Wujing as disciplined and reliable.
- His personality should contrast sharply with Bajie's impulsiveness.

### SHA-02 — The Broken Vessel

- The incident that causes his exile.
- Focus on guilt and consequences rather than spectacle.

### SHA-03 — The Flowing Sands River

- The river becomes a character in itself.
- Wujing survives through repetition and isolation.
- Environmental storytelling replaces exposition where possible.

### SHA-04 — The Travelers Who Never Return

- Wujing encounters passing travelers.
- He becomes increasingly distrustful of promises of rescue.

### SHA-05 — The Pilgrims

- Tang's group reaches the river.
- Wujing initially treats them as another threat.
- Several failed crossing attempts create a mini-arc rather than a single fight.

### SHA-06 — The Offer

- Guanyin's promise of redemption.
- Tang offers Wujing a purpose instead of merely asking for help.

### SHA-07 — Battle Beneath the River

- Major recruitment battle.
- Water-state combat mechanic introduced.
- Wujing's endurance becomes the key to victory.

### SHA-08 — The Quiet Fourth

- Wujing joins the party.
- First full camp conversation with Tang, Wukong and Bajie.
- He becomes the stabilizing presence in the group.

**Milestone:** `SHA_WUJING_RECRUITED`

# 3. Canonical Recruitment Sequence

The party expansion follows this backbone:

```text
Wukong origin
      ↓
Wukong imprisoned
      ↓
Tang origin / pilgrimage begins
      ↓
Tang reaches Five Elements Mountain
      ↓
Wukong joins
      ↓
Bai Longma joins as the mount
      ↓
Gaojiazhuang
      ↓
Zhu Bajie joins
      ↓
Flowing Sands River
      ↓
Sha Wujing joins
      ↓
Five-member party established
```

The exact number of intermediate trials can change, but these recruitment anchors should not drift casually.

# 4. The First Full-Party Chapter

## PARTY-01 — Five Travelers

This is the formal end of the opening structure.

Required party:

- Tang Sanzang
- Sun Wukong
- Zhu Bajie
- Sha Wujing
- Bai Longma

Purpose:

1. Establish the five-person relationship matrix.
2. Let the player freely configure formation.
3. Give every character a small reaction to the road ahead.
4. Introduce the first problem that requires all five character identities.
5. Confirm that the game has transitioned from five origin stories into one JRPG party.

Reward/milestone:

`PARTY_FULL`

---

# 5. The Memory System After Recruitment

After a character joins, their story does **not** vanish.

At camp, inns, temples, special NPCs and chapter milestones, the player can unlock **Memory Chapters**.

A Memory Chapter is a playable flashback/perspective chapter placed at an earlier `historical_timeline_index`.

Example:

```text
Current timeline: after Sha Wujing joins

Camp → Bajie remembers Tianpeng
      → enter BAJ-01 flashback
      → complete memory
      → return to current timeline
```

This gives the game the Octopath-style pleasure of discovering each character's history without making the world chronology inconsistent.

Memory chapters can contain:

- character-specific maps;
- unique enemies;
- flashback-only bosses;
- old abilities;
- dialogue that changes the player's understanding of the present.

Memory chapters must never retroactively alter already-established canonical events unless explicitly marked as an alternate-history or dream sequence.

---

# 6. Shared Journey After the Opening

Once `PARTY_FULL` is reached, new major chapters are generally shared chapters.

Suggested macro flow:

### Shared Act I — The New Party

- early pilgrimage trials;
- first camp conflicts;
- party formation tutorial;
- first multi-character boss.

### Shared Act II — The Road Deepens

- Black Rooster Kingdom;
- Yellow Wind Ridge;
- White Bone Demon;
- major character relationship development.

### Shared Act III — The World Pushes Back

- larger regional arcs;
- increasingly complex enemy factions;
- stronger personal flashbacks;
- relationship-linked combat techniques.

### Shared Act IV — The Cost of the Journey

- characters confront what they originally wanted;
- Heaven, demons and human institutions are no longer simple good/evil categories;
- the pilgrimage begins to feel like a test of the whole group.

### Final Act — One Journey

The five personal themes converge:

- Wukong decides what freedom means when he chooses responsibility.
- Tang discovers that faith does not require blindness.
- Bajie accepts responsibility without losing his humanity.
- Wujing forgives himself without forgetting his past.
- Longma chooses his identity rather than merely accepting duty.

The finale belongs to the party, not to one protagonist.

# 7. Starting-Character Rules

Every new game starts with:

```text
Choose protagonist
      ↓
Load that protagonist's origin route
      ↓
Progress until route reaches a canonical convergence gate
      ↓
Unlock the relevant shared timeline
      ↓
Continue in the global chronology
```

The starting protagonist changes:

- opening chapter;
- first tutorial;
- initial equipment;
- initial abilities;
- early lore perspective;
- first emotional hook;
- which character the player understands most deeply.

The starting protagonist does **not** change:

- canonical recruitment order;
- world milestone IDs;
- historical timeline;
- identity of major shared events.

# 8. Chapter Implementation Contract

Every chapter asset must eventually declare:

```text
chapter_id
owner_character_id
timeline_index
is_origin
is_memory
is_shared
prerequisites
required_world_milestones
completion_milestone
scene_ids
map_ids
encounter_ids
reward_ids
```

No chapter should embed party recruitment logic directly inside scene scripts.

Narrative progression belongs to the narrative state system; combat progression belongs to the combat system.

---

# 9. Content Authoring Rule

The team should write this game in the following order:

1. Lock the five origin outlines.
2. Lock recruitment chapters and canonical milestones.
3. Lock the first full-party chapter.
4. Write the next shared journey arc.
5. Only then expand individual memory chapters.
6. Convert approved chapters into maps, encounters, dialogue and assets.

Do not build dozens of maps before the chapter order is stable.
